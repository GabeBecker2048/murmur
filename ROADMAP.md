# Roadmap

What is planned, in order. Dates are intentions, not commitments, and the
contents of a version may change before it ships. Shipped versions will be in
`CHANGELOG.md`.

## 1.x - possible updates for the future

- Hugging Face mirror: the daily job pushes the day's files, with the layout on HF
  matching `/data/`. Perhaps Murmur's own tables only (index, companies,
  correlations, findings), linking back for headlines, sentiment and prices: a
  takedown on HF means rewriting the repository's history.
- A Zenodo DOI for the `first-year` findings snapshot.

- Reddit and SEC 8-K as sources.
- A larger universe (250, then 500) once the pipeline is boring.
- Intraday prices, if a source allows it, to tighten the windows.
